Register & Login

First time on the server? Follow these steps to get started

▣ Register
New players must register with a password to secure their account. Do this immediately when you first join.
Command
/register <password> <password>
Type your password twice to confirm it.
Example
/register mypassword123 mypassword123
Tips
!
Use a strong password that you will remember.
!
Never share your password with other players or staff.
!
You can change it later with /changepassword.
▣ Login
Every time you join the server you must log in with your password before you can play.
Command
/login <password>
Type the password you used when registering.
Example
/login mypassword123
Other Commands
1
/changepassword <old> <new> — Change your password.
2
/logout — Log out of your account safely.
3
If you forgot your password, open a ticket on our Discord.

Server Rules

Breaking the rules will result in punishment. Ignorance is not an excuse.

01
No Hacking or Cheating
Using hacked clients, X-ray, auto-clickers, macros or any unfair advantage is strictly forbidden. This includes any modification that gives you an advantage over other players.
PERMANENT BAN
02
No Griefing or Stealing
Destroying other players' builds or stealing their items without permission is not allowed. Always respect other players' property and claimed land.
BAN
03
Respect All Players
Harassment, racism, hate speech, bullying or toxic behavior of any kind is not tolerated. Treat everyone with respect in chat, voice and all server channels.
MUTE BAN
04
No Spamming or Advertising
Do not spam the chat, send repeated messages or advertise other servers, Discord servers or websites without permission from staff.
MUTE
05
No Scamming
Scamming other players in trades, auctions or any in-game deal is forbidden. All agreed trades must be honored. Real-money trades are done at your own risk.
BAN
06
No Inappropriate Builds or Names
Building offensive structures or using inappropriate usernames, nicknames or signs is not allowed. Staff may remove builds and punish accordingly.
WARNING BAN
07
Respect Staff Decisions
Arguing with staff or evading punishments (ban evasion with alt accounts) will result in harsher consequences. If you disagree with a punishment, appeal on Discord.
PERMANENT BAN
08
English or Greek in Main Chat
Please use only English or Greek in the main chat so that everyone can understand. Other languages are fine in private messages.
WARNING
